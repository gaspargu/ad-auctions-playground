# Ad Auctions Playground

Study and research repository on advertising auction design, working toward a proof of concept of the **Information-Bundling Position Auction (IBPA)** and a potential thesis topic.

## Current status

Currently focused on reading and understanding the paper. There is no implementation, no experiments have been run, and no original results have been produced yet. The implementation language and tools will be chosen later.

## Reference paper

Srinivas Tunuguntla, Carl F. Mela, and Jason Pratt. _Designing Ad Auctions with Targeting Information_. August 13, 2026 version, SSRN 6050614.

- [SSRN record](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6050614)
- [BibTeX bibliography](references/bibliography.bib)

The date identifies the local version being studied; future versions may change equations, numbering, or results. The paper's findings have not yet been reproduced by this project.
